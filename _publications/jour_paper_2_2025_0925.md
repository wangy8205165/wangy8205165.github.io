Isolation Forest (iForest) is a foundational technique for unsupervised anomaly detection, valued for its efficiency, simplicity, and strong performance on unlabeled data. Over time, iForest has grown into a diverse family of methods—including classical, streaming, high-dimensional, hybrid, deep-learning, and explainable variants.

This review surveys these developments with a focus on real-world fraud detection. While iForest remains an efficient and lightweight approach, its practical impact in fraud scenarios is constrained by limited transparency, insufficient support for streaming data, and a lack of nuanced treatment of fraud-specific patterns. Drawing on a wide range of recent studies, we highlight these gaps and outline key research directions to better align iForest methodologies with the complex requirements of modern fraud detection.
